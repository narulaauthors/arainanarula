ISHIMIKO

A mesmerizing visual story where mystical places, mysterious portals, extraordinary characters, and the power of friendship come together in a thrilling adventure.

Ishimiko is a curious and adventurous girl whose ordinary day changes forever when she discovers a glowing portal leading to a hidden magical forest. What begins as a place filled with wonder soon reveals mysteries beyond her imagination. Who are its extraordinary inhabitants, how did they arrive in this whimsical world, and what secrets do these lands hold? Join Ishimiko on her journey to uncover the answers.
